I'm a UI/UX & Product Designer who turns ideas into interfaces that feel simple, clear, and easy to use. I focus on usability, visual hierarchy, and consistency, so every screen has a purpose.

I take products from concept to polished experience: user flows, wireframes, UI design, prototyping, and iteration. As a frontend developer working in Figma, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, I design with a clear sense of how things get built, so my work translates smoothly from mockup to working product.

Outside of design, I explore robotics, drones, and IoT. They keep me curious about how technology and thoughtful design can come together to build better products.
